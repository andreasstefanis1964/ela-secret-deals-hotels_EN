What are cookies?
A cookie is a small file placed on the hard drive of your computer. You may refuse to accept browser cookies by activating the appropriate settings on your browser. However, if you select this setting you may be unable to access certain parts of our Website, App or Service. Unless you have adjusted your browser setting so that it will refuse cookies, our system will issue cookies when you direct your browser to our Website, App or Service.
When you visit our Website or App some or all of the following types of cookies may be set on your device:
Necessary cookies:

These cookies are strictly necessary to provide you with services available through our Website and to use some of its features, such as access to secure areas, as well as data protection.

Preference cookies:
These cookies are used to store your preferences and various settings, enabling our Website to remember your choices (such as your user name, language, or the region you are in) and provide enhanced, more personal features. They may also be used to provide services you have asked for.

Marketing cookies:
These cookies enhance your online experience by delivering content tailored to your interests and preferences. They gather insights into your browsing patterns, allowing us to present you with more relevant advertisements and promotions that resonate with your unique tastes. By understanding user behavior, these cookies help us connect you with services and products that align best with your needs, ensuring a more personalized web experience.

What about other tracking technologies, like web beacons?

We do not employ other tracking technologies such as web beacons, also known as clear GIFs or pixel tags.
